## Blocking

- Frozen v0.3 text is not in the tree, only a claude.ai link.
- Neighbour diagram is an embedded-content placeholder.
- OPERATOR_EMAIL, OLLAMA_BASE_URL, and SERPAPI_KEY are unset, and ports are 'to confirm'.
- Shopify review source is 'app to confirm', so the phrase-source enum is not closed.
- S1 proof requires four clean weeks, a discovered Watch term, and a human-cleared Rulings queue.
- Probe 1 requires identical SerpApi values an hour apart.
- S1 requires apparel_trends.json while idea_text is specified only for S2.
- Probe 5 can demand an Amp or Reverb endpoint that Open call 5 has not allowed.

## Clauses no test could check

- Shirts that hit people in the gut.
- Every arrow on the right ends at a person, while the diagram is missing.
- The team does not read text reports.
- What makes the ledger credible to a Labs prospect versus a toy, beyond the mechanical rules already tested.
- Scout stays out of named brand judgment calls, except as a prohibition on writing those decisions.
- Grok review prompt and the FINE/SHOULD/MUST ritual.
- First prod night on or after Dec 2 and not the same night as another first night.
- Days-left and plan renewal wherever an API happens to report them, with no field named.
- Reuse of Amp and Reverb incident history as correctness evidence.
